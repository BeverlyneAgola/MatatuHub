ABOUT US

About MatatuHub

A centralized management information system designed for matatu Sacco operations.

Who We Are

MatatuHub is a management information system designed to support the day-to-day management of a matatu Sacco and its employees.

The system brings together information from different areas of the organization, allowing authorized staff to manage records and operations through a centralized platform.

What We Aim To Achieve

MatatuHub aims to improve the organization, accessibility and accountability of information used in matatu operations.

By keeping important records within one system, management can make better use of operational information while maintaining appropriate access controls.

Our Approach

The system is organized around the different responsibilities within the Sacco, including human resources, finance, fleet management, operations and driver and conductor activities.

Access to information is controlled according to the responsibilities of each user.
