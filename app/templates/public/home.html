WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Vision, Mission and Purpose

Our Vision

To support efficient, organized and accountable matatu operations through accessible information systems.

Our Mission

To provide a centralized platform that improves the management of people, vehicles, operations and organizational information.

Our Purpose

To make important operational information easier to manage, access and use for better decision-making.
